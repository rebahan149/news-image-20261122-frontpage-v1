## Heroes (drawn, 1–3)

1. **Kyiv region, Ukraine — drone barrage.** Russia launched 186
   attack/decoy drones overnight into 22 Nov (72 jet-powered);
   Ukrainian air defence downed or suppressed 161; strikes recorded
   at 18 locations. A drone destroyed one stairwell section of a
   five-storey block in Dymer, north of Kyiv; rescuers searched the
   rubble for trapped residents. Sources: Ukrainian Air Force via
   Reuters; Euromaidan Press; Kyiv Independent wires.
2. **Antalya, Türkiye — COP31 ends with no deal.** The longest UN
   climate summit on record closed Sunday without adopting its cover
   decision: the final draft omitted any mention of fossil fuels,
   the EU and UK refused to endorse it, and COP31 President Murat
   Kurum conceded "there is no agreement on this text." First failed
   adoption since COP6 (2000). Draft elements noted on the page's
   reporting: 35%-electricity-by-2035 goal and a first formal
   acknowledgement of >1.5 °C overshoot — both in the unadopted text.
   Sources: AP, Reuters, BBC, The Times, phys.org/AFP.
3. **Manama, Bahrain — radar claim vs denial.** The IRGC claimed it
   destroyed two US Patriot radars on Saturday night. Bahrain's
   foreign ministry responded Sunday: the strike hit a civilian
   industrial site, caused a fire and no injuries, and violated
   Bahrain's sovereignty. Labelled CLAIM on the page. Sources:
   Khaleej Times, Al Jazeera.
